What to Expect During Your USCIS Naturalization Interview: Step-by-Step Guide

Published by OmniCitizen Editorial Team • October 2026 • 6 min read

Passing the USCIS naturalization interview is the final major hurdle before taking your Oath of Allegiance and becoming a United States citizen. Knowing what to expect can alleviate anxiety and ensure you arrive fully prepared.

1. Reviewing Your Form N-400 Application

The officer will go through your Form N-400 line by line to verify your personal history, employment, travel records, and background questions. Make sure you review your submitted copy thoroughly beforehand.

2. English Speaking, Reading, and Writing Tests

Your ability to speak English is assessed from the moment you greet the officer. The reading and writing portions require you to read aloud one out of three sentences correctly and write one dictation sentence correctly.

3. The Oral Civics Test

The civics test is administered orally. The officer will ask up to 10 questions from the official bank. Once you answer 6 correctly, the civics portion concludes successfully. Practicing with online mock exam simulators is a great way to build confidence.

4. Managing Preparation and Documentation
