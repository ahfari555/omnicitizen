About this website

About OmniCitizen

An independent educational resource for naturalization civics study.

Our purpose

OmniCitizen is designed to help learners prepare for the U.S. naturalization civics test through clear study guidance, explanatory articles, and browser-based practice activities. The goal is to make civics topics easier to review and to encourage learners to understand the ideas behind the questions—not only memorize short answers.

What you will find here

  • Test-version guidance: introductory information about the 2008 and 2025 USCIS civics test formats, with links to official updates.
  • Practice activities: a limited learning sample with answer explanations. It is not represented as a complete official USCIS question bank.
  • Study articles: practical guidance on building a study plan, preparing for the naturalization interview, U.S. history and government, citizenship rights and responsibilities, and interview documents.
  • Official references: links to USCIS and other government resources so visitors can verify current requirements.

Independent and not affiliated with USCIS

OmniCitizen is an independent website. It is not operated by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the Department of Homeland Security, or any U.S. government agency. The name and references to government programs are used only to describe the subject of the educational material.

Accuracy and limitations

Naturalization test rules, accepted answers, and current-officeholder answers can change. We make reasonable efforts to keep educational content useful, but we cannot guarantee that every page is complete or current at all times. Always confirm the test version and requirements that apply to your own application using current official USCIS notices and study materials.

Practice questions and scores are learning aids only. They are not official test questions unless specifically identified as such, are not official USCIS test results, and do not predict an interview outcome.
