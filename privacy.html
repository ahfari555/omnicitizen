Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

OmniCitizen is an independent educational website offering articles and practice activities for people preparing for the U.S. naturalization civics test. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit omnicitizen.vercel.app.

Information used by practice tools

The civics practice activities are designed to run in your browser. Answers and practice results are used to show feedback on the page. The site is not designed to request Social Security numbers, A-Numbers, immigration case numbers, passport details, or other sensitive identity information. Do not enter sensitive personal information into free-text fields.

Hosting and security infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and error or access logs. Retention depends on the hosting provider and its settings.

Cookies and advertising

OmniCitizen may display Google AdSense advertisements on editorial article pages. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalize, and measure advertising, subject to location, consent choices, and Google's policies. Learn more at Google partner-site privacy information and manage preferences at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party resources

The website loads resources from third parties such as Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS CDN. Your browser may connect directly to those providers. External links, including USCIS and other government resources, are governed by their own privacy policies.

How information is used

  • Deliver website pages and educational practice features.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.
  • Display and measure advertising where enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

OmniCitizen does not sell personal information submitted directly to it. The website does not currently describe a user account or membership system.

Children's privacy

The site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact the site operator if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

Retention and security

Practice answers are intended to be processed in the browser rather than submitted to an OmniCitizen account. Technical logs and data handled by third parties may be retained under their systems and policies. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can control cookies through browser settings and available Google consent or advertising controls. Blocking cookies or third-party resources may affect ads or some website features.

Policy changes

This policy may change when website features, advertising arrangements, or legal requirements change. Review the updated date above periodically.

Contact

Use the Contact page for privacy questions. The site operator must ensure it provides a working contact method before publication.

Important: This is a general disclosure draft, not legal advice. The operator should confirm actual hosting settings, AdSense consent configuration, cookies, and all scripts before publishing. Do not claim that no data is collected if hosting or advertising providers process technical information.
