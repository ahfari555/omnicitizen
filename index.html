Prepare for Your U.S. Citizenship Civics Test

Clear study guidance for the USCIS 2008 and 2025 civics tests. Identify the test version that applies to your Form N-400 filing date, review official study materials, and strengthen your understanding with practice.

2008 Civics Test

100-question bank

Generally applies if you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025.

  • Up to 10 questions asked orally
  • At least 6 correct answers to pass
  • Special 65/20 consideration may apply

2025 Civics Test

128-question bank

Generally applies if you filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025.

  • Up to 20 questions asked orally
  • At least 12 correct answers to pass
  • Test stops after 12 correct or 9 incorrect
Official USCIS 2025 question list ↗

Which test should I study?

USCIS determines the test version by the date you filed Form N-400, not by the date you visit this website. Check your filing confirmation and compare it with the dates above. Some applicants may qualify for exceptions or special consideration; verify your situation directly with USCIS.

Important accuracy note

Some civics answers change after elections or appointments. Questions asking for current officeholders, state officials, or local representatives require up-to-date answers. Always check the official USCIS test updates shortly before your interview.
